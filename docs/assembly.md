# Sablefin SSP-4 assembly

1. Fit the TDR cell into the lower housing and tighten the two M8 glands.
2. Mount the controller board on the four standoffs; do not exceed 0.6 Nm.
3. Connect the battery holder and verify 3.3 V on TP2 before sealing.
4. Flash `sablefin-ssp4.hex` and run the field calibration procedure.
5. Pressure-test the housing at 0.5 bar for ten minutes.
